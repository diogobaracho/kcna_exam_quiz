# Question

What is a Service Level Indicator (SLI)?

## Options

- [x] A specific, measured metric that quantifies some aspect of the service's behavior, like request latency or error rate
- [ ] A legal contract between a provider and a customer
- [ ] A target percentage the team promises to hit
- [ ] A dashboard tool used to visualize logs

## Explanation

An SLI is the actual measurement (e.g. 'percentage of requests under 300ms'); a Service Level Objective (SLO) sets a target for that SLI, and a Service Level Agreement (SLA) is the business/contractual commitment often based on the SLO.

## Explicação para criança

É o número real medido, tipo o termômetro mostrando a temperatura agora, diferente da meta que você quer alcançar ou do contrato que promete aquela meta.

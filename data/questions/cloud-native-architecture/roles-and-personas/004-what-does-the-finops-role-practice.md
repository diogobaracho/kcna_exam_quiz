# Question

What does the FinOps role/practice focus on in a cloud native organization?

## Options

- [x] Bringing financial accountability to variable cloud spend by fostering collaboration between engineering, finance, and business teams
- [ ] Auditing source code for security vulnerabilities
- [ ] Managing employee payroll systems
- [ ] Writing Kubernetes networking policies

## Explanation

FinOps introduces practices and shared visibility so engineering teams understand the cost impact of their infrastructure decisions in real time, enabling data-driven trade-offs between cost, speed, and quality rather than cost being discovered only after the fact.

## Explicação para criança

É como ter alguém que ajuda a família a entender e conversar sobre a conta de luz variável, para todo mundo decidir junto onde economizar sem brigar.

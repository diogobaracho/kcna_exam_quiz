# Question

Why does vendor-neutral, open governance matter for a project like Kubernetes?

## Options

- [x] It prevents any single company from unilaterally controlling the project's direction, encouraging broader trust and contribution
- [ ] It guarantees the software will always be free of bugs
- [ ] It means only volunteers, never companies, may contribute
- [ ] It requires every decision to be unanimous

## Explanation

Open governance under a neutral foundation means decisions are made through documented, community-driven processes rather than one vendor's roadmap, which encourages competitors to collaborate and users to trust the project's long-term direction.

## Explicação para criança

É como um jogo com regras escritas que valem para todo mundo igual, em vez de um jogador dono da bola que pode mudar as regras quando quiser.
